# PAXECT Enterprise Pack Overview

This document outlines the **Enterprise Pack** vision for the PAXECT ecosystem.  
It clarifies which components are fully open source today and what may become part of future enterprise-grade extensions.

---

## Scope

The **open-source core** will always remain free and licensed under Apache-2.0:

- **PAXECT Core** — deterministic runtime  
- **AEAD Hybrid Plugin** — secure AEAD bridge  
- **Polyglot Plugin** — cross-language data flow  
- **SelfTune Plugin** — adaptive runtime control  
- **Link Plugin** — relay and policy layer  
- **Demo Suite (01–10)** and CI matrix  

The **Enterprise Pack** introduces optional extensions for organizations requiring  
operational support, integration tooling, or compliance automation.

---

## Vision

The Enterprise Pack is designed to **extend** PAXECT — not replace it.  
It focuses on the following areas:

- Advanced **security integration** (HSM / KMS / Vault)  
- Extended **policy and audit** engine  
- **Monitoring connectors** (Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, Kafka)  
- **Deployment assets** (systemd units, Helm charts, Docker images)  
- **Compliance documentation** (ISO / IEC / NIST mappings)  
- Optional **priority support** through verified partners  

---

## Community & Ownership

- The **PAXECT Core** remains fully open source.  
- The **Enterprise Pack** is proprietary to the project Owner.  
- Verified partners or maintainers may co-develop or maintain enterprise extensions.  
- No vendor lock-in: organizations can always use the Core independently.  

---

## Participation

At this stage:

- PAXECT does **not sell or distribute** enterprise packages.  
- **Volunteers and partners** are welcome to collaborate on early testing or feature proposals.  
- For integration discussions, use **GitHub Discussions → Ideas**.

---

## Key Principle

> **Open Core. Verified Extensions. Zero Telemetry by Default.**

The goal is to maintain a deterministic, private, and reproducible foundation  
for both community users and enterprise environments.
